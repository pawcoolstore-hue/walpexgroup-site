Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Walpex Analytics is operated by Walpex Group Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use Walpex Analytics and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Walpex Analytics may process information required to provide analytics, reporting and business insights. This may include website activity, traffic information, advertising performance data, store information, and other information made available through connected services.

2. Connected Services

Walpex Analytics may connect with third-party services such as Google Analytics, advertising platforms and Shopify. Information received from these services is used to provide analytics, reporting and related business functionality.

3. Google Analytics

Walpex Analytics may use Google Analytics data to provide reporting and analytics features. The information available to the application depends on the permissions granted by the user and the configuration of the connected Google Analytics property.

4. Advertising Data

When advertising platforms are connected, Walpex Analytics may process advertising performance information, including campaign, advertising account and performance metrics, for reporting and analysis.

5. Shopify Data

When a Shopify store is connected, Walpex Analytics may process store information and order or product-related data necessary to provide analytics and reporting functionality.

6. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide, maintain and improve Walpex Analytics, generate reports, analyse business performance, troubleshoot technical issues and provide requested functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed through the service against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the application and provide its functionality, or where required by law.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of your information, please contact Walpex Group Ltd through the contact information available on our website.
