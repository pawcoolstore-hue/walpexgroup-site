Data Deletion Instructions

Walpex Analytics · Effective date: August 16, 2026

How to request deletion

If you want Walpex Analytics to delete data associated with your connected account, send a request to the contact email published on the Walpex website. Include the email address used for your Walpex Analytics account and, if applicable, the connected store or advertising account name.

Meta data

If you connected a Meta account to Walpex Analytics and want the Meta-related data removed, request deletion using the process above. We will process the request and remove applicable data held by Walpex Analytics, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes.

Disconnecting an account

You may also disconnect a connected third-party service from Walpex Analytics. Disconnecting a service stops future access to that service's data, subject to the technical operation of the integration.

Third-party data

This request does not delete data held directly by Meta, Shopify, Google or another third-party service. For deletion of data held by those providers, use their respective account and privacy controls.

Contact

For a deletion request, contact Walpex Group Ltd through the contact details published on the Walpex website.
